What is stored

  • Account. Your email address, handle, display name, optional bio, country and avatar, plus a password hash if you sign in with a password. If the instance uses an external login provider, the identifier that provider gives us is stored instead.
  • Activity. The check-ins you post: drink, rating, notes, photos, venue, time and the visibility you chose. Comments, lists, badges, saves and friend connections are stored the same way.
  • Technical. A session cookie so you stay signed in, the IP address each request comes from (used to rate-limit and to keep logs), API tokens when you create one (stored only as a hash), and push subscriptions if you enable notifications.
    • {{if .Legal.Analytics}}
  • Analytics. This instance runs a self-hosted Umami instance at {{.Legal.AnalyticsURL}}. It records page views without cookies and without a persistent identifier, and you can switch it off entirely under Settings → Privacy.
    • {{end}} {{if .Legal.Traces}}
  • Server traces. OpenTelemetry tracing is enabled on this instance. Traces describe request timings on the server and are not used to build a profile of you.
    • {{end}} {{if .Legal.OIDC}}
  • Login provider. When you sign in through the configured identity provider, that provider tells us who you are and we store the link between the two accounts.
    • {{end}}

Why it is stored

  • To run your account — signing you in, showing your check-ins to the audience you picked, and keeping your data when you come back. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • To keep the instance secure — rate limiting, abuse prevention and diagnosing failures. Legal basis: legitimate interest in a working, safe service.
  • To keep records we are required to keep — for example a note that an account was closed at your request. Legal basis: legal obligation.
    • {{if .Legal.Analytics}}
  • To understand usage — aggregate page views only. Legal basis: legitimate interest, and you can object at any time by switching analytics off in Settings → Privacy.
    • {{end}}

Who else sees it

Your check-ins are visible to the audience you choose for each one: only you, your friends, or anyone with an account. Nobody else gets a copy of your data, and it is never sold.

Processors that may handle it on our behalf:

    {{if .Legal.Mail}}
  • An email provider, which delivers verification and password-reset messages and therefore sees your address and the message content.
    • {{end}} {{if .Legal.Push}}
  • Push services (Google Firebase or the browser vendor's push service), which deliver notifications to your device if you enable them. They receive a device token, not your account details.
    • {{end}} {{if .Legal.OIDC}}
  • Your identity provider, which authenticates you and returns your identity to us.
    • {{end}}
  • The hosting provider running this server and its backups.
  • Open Food Facts, which is queried with a barcode when a product is unknown. No account information is sent.

Some of these may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, transfers rely on an adequacy decision or the standard contractual clauses of the provider.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets one cookie, {{.Legal.SessionCookie}}, and only when you sign in. It holds an opaque session token, is HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and exists so you do not have to sign in on every page. It is deleted when you sign out; the matching server-side session is deleted when you sign out everywhere.

There are no advertising cookies, no third-party tracking scripts and no cross-site pixels.{{if .Legal.Analytics}} The analytics above is self-hosted and cookieless.{{else}} No analytics service is enabled on this instance.{{end}} Because nothing here is optional beyond the sign-in cookie, the site does not show a consent banner — and it does not need one.

How long it is kept

Account data is kept while your account exists. Sessions expire on their own, and old rate-limit and request logs are discarded as the logs rotate.

When you delete your account, your email address, handle, display name, bio, country, avatar and password are erased immediately, and every session, token, push subscription and share link is revoked. Check-ins, comments and photos you posted stay, detached from any identity and shown as a deleted user, because friends' feeds and other people's conversations reference them. More detail is on Delete your account.

Your rights

  • Get a copy. Download your data as JSON from your account, or GET /api/v1/me/export in the app.
  • Correct it. Most fields are editable in Settings.
  • Delete it. Use Delete your account. It takes effect immediately, without a waiting period.
  • Object or restrict. Turn analytics off, change the visibility of your check-ins, or ask us to restrict processing.
  • Complain. You can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in the country where you live or work.

Anything not covered by the self-service pages can be requested from the contact above; we answer within one month, as the GDPR requires.
