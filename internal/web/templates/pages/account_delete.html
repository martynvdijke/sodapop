Account

Delete your account

Deletion is immediate. Your identity is erased the moment you confirm; nothing is held for a waiting period.

Delete it yourself

{{if .User}}

You are signed in as {{.User.Handle}}. Go to Settings, open Delete account, and type your handle to confirm.

{{else}}

Sign in, then go to Settings → Delete account and type your handle to confirm. Sign in if you have not already.

{{end}}

In the Android app the same action lives under Settings → Account → Delete account, and it calls the same endpoint with the same confirmation.

If you cannot sign in

{{if .Legal.ContactEmail}}

Write to {{.Legal.ContactEmail}} from the address on the account and ask for it to be deleted. Requests are handled within one month, usually much sooner.

{{else}}

Ask the administrator of this instance to delete the account for you. They can do it from the admin tools; there is no self-service route that works without signing in.

{{end}}

What is removed

  • Your email address, handle, display name, bio, country and avatar.
  • Your password, and every way into the account: browser sessions, API tokens, webhook tokens, push subscriptions and share links.
  • Your link to an external login provider, if you used one.
  • Your friend connections, follows and blocks, and your notifications.

What stays

Check-ins, comments and photos you posted remain, because other people's feeds and conversations refer to them. They are detached from your identity and appear as a deleted user, so they cannot be traced back to you.

Backups taken before the deletion may still contain the old data until they rotate out; they are not used to restore individual accounts.

The full detail is in the privacy policy.
